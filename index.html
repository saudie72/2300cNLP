Try a check-in

Tell us how today feels, in your own words

Type a sentence or two. A small language model reads it and suggests a Low, Steady, or Strong session. You can always choose a different one.

Runs entirely in your browser; what you type is not sent or saved. This is a suggestion tool, not a medical assessment.

Your suggestion will appear here.

Try one of the examples, or write your own.

How this works (for the NLP-curious)

Your text is split into words and word pairs, weighted with TF-IDF, and scored by a logistic regression classifier trained in Python (scikit-learn) on 660 synthetic check-in messages. A Naive Bayes model was trained as a baseline.

On 60 separately written test messages it is about 90% accurate. Because suggesting too much is worse than too little, the page picks the gentler option when the model is unsure. Messages that mention possible warning signs (such as chest pain, fever, or new arm swelling) get a stop-and-check message instead of a session.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Strong Again for?

Strong Again is designed for women going through or beyond breast cancer who want movement support that takes changing energy and post-treatment experiences into account.

Do I need to be an athlete?

No particular fitness level is required. Choose the options that fit you, and take the program at your own pace.

What if a movement doesn't feel right?

Pause or stop, and use only options that are appropriate for you. Strong Again provides general fitness guidance—not individualized medical advice. Check with your care team before starting an exercise program and follow their guidance.

Is Strong Again medical care or physical therapy?

No. Strong Again is a fitness and movement resource. It does not replace medical care, physical therapy, or advice from your healthcare professionals.

Do I have to follow the running path?

No. The return-to-running path is an optional feature for people whose own goal includes running. It is not required to use the program.
